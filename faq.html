    Product

    Answers about research-use-only requirements, testing, product appearance, peptide form, storage, and temperature exposure.

    Research Use Only / Not for Human Consumption

    Ion Peptide products are for laboratory research use only. They are not intended for human or animal consumption and are not designed to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. These products are not to be used as food, drugs, cosmetics, or household items.

    By accessing this website or purchasing from Ion Peptide, you agree that compliance with all applicable laws and regulations is your sole responsibility. No information provided should be considered medical advice.

    Please review our Terms of Service.

    Product Testing

    Ion Peptide verifies every batch of every product through accredited, independent third-party laboratories. Because each compound requires specialized analytical methods, we work closely with qualified laboratories and PhD-level experts in analytical and organic chemistry to develop appropriate testing protocols.

    Analytical methods may include NMR, FTIR, HPLC, LC-MS, GC-MS, TLC, ICP-MS, gravimetry, UV-Vis spectroscopy, titration, melting point analysis, and organoleptic evaluation. Testing primarily focuses on purity, content, sterility, and endotoxin/LPS levels.

    Testing is conducted by Freedom Diagnostics in Franklin, Tennessee. Lab results are available at Lab Results.

    Product Appearance Variations

    Natural variations in color, texture, odor, or biochemical properties may occur and do not indicate reduced quality or purity.

    Peptide Form

    Peptides are supplied in lyophilized powder form to enhance stability and extend shelf life during storage and transit.

    Peptide Top Colors

    Product images may show white vial tops for consistency. Actual vial top colors may vary.

    Peptide Powder Appearance

    Lyophilized peptides typically form a solid cake. Movement during transit may cause the cake to crack or appear uneven. This is an aesthetic variation and does not affect quality, potency, or purity.

    Certain peptides and proteins may appear larger due to buffering requirements. This is normal and does not indicate a variance in content.

    Storage Instructions

    General storage: keep products in a cool, dry place away from heat, moisture, and direct sunlight.

    Best practice: refrigeration or freezing is recommended to maintain long-term stability.

    Extreme Temperature Exposure

    All molecules degrade over time, but proper storage greatly slows this process.

    Low temperatures are generally safe and may cause temporary precipitation without increasing degradation. Prolonged high temperatures can accelerate degradation.

    Brief temperature excursions, such as short-term heat exposure or less than 24 hours at room temperature for frozen products, do not typically cause measurable degradation when the product is returned to proper storage, unless combined with exposure to oxygen, moisture, or sunlight.

    Account and Orders

    Answers about order statuses, order history, confirmation emails, lost passwords, returns, refund timing, zero-tolerance rules, and blacklisting.

    Order Status

    Payment Pending: we are awaiting your payment. Your order did not process with BarterPay or Grid ACH.

    On Hold: we are awaiting or verifying your payment. If your order remains in this status, please submit a photo of your payment confirmation. This status is related to BarterPay and ACH.

    Processing: your payment has been confirmed, and your order is being prepared for shipment. Orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours, though processing times may vary.

    Completed: your order has been dispatched. You will receive an email with your tracking number shortly.

    Cancelled: we could not verify your payment, resulting in order cancellation. If you have made a payment and see this status, please send a photo of your payment confirmation.

    Order History

    Order history is only available for registered users. Guest orders become visible once you create an account using the same email used at checkout.

    Return / Refund Policy

    Returns are accepted within 30 days for unopened, unused items in original sealed packaging. Opened or used items are not eligible.

    Start your return through the Return Portal before mailing anything back. The portal connects the request to the correct order and eligible item or items. Customers pay return shipping unless the error is ours. Expedited shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

    Once received, returns are typically inspected and processed within 3–5 business days. If approved, please allow up to 10 business days from the date we receive your return for the refund process to be completed.

    Please review our Refund / Return Policy.
