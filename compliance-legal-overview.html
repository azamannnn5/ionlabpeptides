    Operational Standards

    Built for research-only transparency

    This overview should feel more like a formal compliance dossier than a simple policy page. The goal is to clearly show how Ion Peptide defines product use, labels products, supports documentation, communicates boundaries, and monitors for misuse.

    Research-focused classification

    Ion Peptide products are not presented for clinical, therapeutic, diagnostic, compounding, dietary supplement, cosmetic, human, or veterinary use.

    Documentation-backed operations

    Certificates of Analysis are available for download on product listings, and shipments include printed or digital access to relevant COA documentation.

    Clear misuse boundaries

    Ion Peptide does not provide dosing, preparation, reconstitution, injection, administration, storage-for-consumption, or usage guidance.

    01

    Company Legal Structure

    IonPeptide.com | Great Research, LLC is a New Mexico-based Limited Liability Company, registered and in good standing. Our business operates exclusively as a research chemical supplier.

    We are not a pharmacy, compounding facility, or health product distributor, and we do not market products for human or veterinary use.

    02

    Intended Use of Products

    All products sold by IonPeptide.com are strictly for laboratory research and analytical purposes.

    They are:

    • Not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
    • Not intended for human or animal consumption or use
    • Not for therapeutic, diagnostic, or medical purposes
    • Not to be compounded, repackaged, or resold as drugs, dietary supplements, or cosmetics

    These restrictions are clearly disclosed on:

    • Our Terms of Service
    • Product labels
    • Checkout disclaimers
    • Packaging inserts
    03

    FDA Compliance — Research Use Only (RUO)

    All products are labeled in accordance with 21 CFR 809.10(c)(2)(i) as Research Use Only (RUO). They are not intended for clinical investigations or diagnostic applications.

    RUO-specific labeling includes:

    • “For Research Use Only”
    • “Not for Human or Animal Use”
    • No dosing, reconstitution, or administration instructions provided
    Ion Peptide’s RUO position is intended to prevent product misclassification, misuse, or interpretation as clinical, therapeutic, diagnostic, or consumer-use products.
    04

    Product Testing & Documentation

    Every product undergoes rigorous testing via HPLC and Mass Spectrometry to confirm identity and purity.

    • Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are available for download on product listings
    • Every shipment includes either a printed COA or a digital link to the relevant COA
    05

    Packaging, Labeling, and Shipment

    • Products are shipped in tamper-evident, RUO-labeled packaging
    • Packaging excludes medically symbolic or injection-related language
    • COAs and RUO disclaimers are included with all shipments
    • Shipments use generic, discreet exterior boxes
    • Any included bacteriostatic water is labeled: “For Laboratory Research Use Only. Not for injection or clinical use.”
    06

    Communication & Customer Support Policy

    IonPeptide.com does not provide guidance on:

    • Dosing
    • Preparation or reconstitution
    • Injection or administration
    • Storage for consumption

    Any inquiries regarding these topics are redirected to our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

    07

    Enforcement, Monitoring & Misuse Reporting

    We actively monitor for product misuse and take appropriate measures.

    • Canceling suspicious or non-research orders
    • Restricting or banning customers who violate Terms
    • Disclaiming responsibility for third-party content
    • Cooperating with authorities if unlawful use is identified
    All customers agree to indemnify IonPeptide.com from misuse-related liability at checkout.

    We maintain this page publicly to promote transparency and reduce the risk of product misclassification.
